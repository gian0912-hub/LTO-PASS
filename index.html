Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a driver's license?

Start with a student permit, then apply for a non-professional license once you are eligible. Most applications go through the LTMS portal. Check the LTO for the current requirements and fees.

How can I renew my driver's license?

Renewals are done through the LTMS portal or at an LTO office. Renew before your license expires, and bring a valid ID and a medical certificate if asked.

Can I use my student permit to drive alone?

No. A student permit only lets you drive with a licensed driver sitting beside you.

How many questions are on the LTO written exam?

The written exam is multiple choice. Our mock exam uses 40 questions so you can practice at the same pace.

How long does the entire licensing process take?

It depends on your license type and how fast you complete each step. A student permit comes first, and you need to hold it for a set period before applying for the next license.

Will I pass?

If you study the topics and keep retaking the practice tests until the explanations make sense, you will be well prepared.

Which license are you applying for?

Every license has its own exam and reviewer. Pick the one that matches where you are today to see what it covers.
